The Next Horizon

The Next Horizon is volume three of The Book of Newts, which follows the adventures and trials of a witch that owns a magical book detailing many secrets of science, engineering, magic and most particularly, space travel.


 After a recent conflict between Amelia Blackwell and The Book of Newts, in which the two talked out their differences, Amelia decided she wants it all: both to settle down and explore space, at the same time. To that end, she lays plans to build a generational star ship, using the granite foundation of a city as the mega-scale star ship’s basis.

To that end, she accepts a knighthood and a very contentious piece of land to rule on behalf of her king: the city of Rimestar, which was once capitol of the nation that forced the Blackwell sisters into military service, as War Witches.

Unfortunately, politics is a thresher and she soon learns she put her thumb right into it, as assassins try to kill her with weapons she designed. Worse, there’s some strange and violent beast hiding in the woods in the foothills of Mount Rimestar. As if those troubles weren’t enough, a nearby star associated with The Fates has gone nova, leading to the rise of a dangerous cult that believes the end times are near, while The Fates become blind to mortal affairs, because Uthec, the Fate of the present day, is in the middle of childbirth.

However, just as politics begin to get really nasty for Amelia, an old general from the Dugaria military starts a rebellion, to seize control of Rimestar, because the soldiers under her command have no desired to be ruled by Dame Amelia Blackwell, whom they view as a usurper.

Will Amelia and her sisters put down the rebels and launch her gigantic star ship, or will the project die before it can ever be completed? Will they survive the crucible of politics, or will they become the slag skimmed off the top for the sake of a new king?

Bonus Content: Rowley’s Roar

Rowley’s Roar is a musical album based on this series, telling parts of the story in song, from the perspective of the pirate, “Roaring” Rowley.

The song that’s most relevant to this volume is Waiting in the Void, which is track 14.

Waiting in the Void covers the state of Rowley during most of the novel and some of the lyrics are quoted in it. Beware of a small spoiler for the story in the song.
