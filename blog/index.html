Friday Feature #3: Scarlet Eye: Homecoming

October 02, 2026 — Owen Tyme

Today's feature is for a piece that became available in stores for the first time, today. Scarlet Eye: Homecoming is volume six of Ashen Blades and volume two of the sub-series, Scarlet Eye.

I waited a long time to write this piece, because I needed to firmly establish the series, before pulling this kind of serious plot thread.

A Little About Mashu'ra

The surface details have been a part of this series from the beginning, staring with the basics: Mashu'ra is a demon, but more specifically, he's a juvenile demon, more commonly known as an imp.

Long before the series began, he was summoned by a demon-witch, Annmarie Nossett, to serve as her familiar. Annmarie happens to be the mother of the main character of the series, The Hunter/Artemis Watson.

He used to be in the form of a full-grown black cat (and quite a large example, at that, probably a Maine Coon), but when his master died, he shrunk back down to the size of a kitten, a fact he found embarrassing.

He's also a very knowledgeable expert on both demons and witches, with unusual insight into demonic politics. In earlier novels, he served as a source of exposition, before the demon-hunting organization he eventually became part of knew much about the way demons thought.

He was also in love with his last master, because she chose to give him his soul. She chose to free him, even though she knew she was doomed to suffer for choosing to become a demon.

And that's the biggest thing that was previously known: he chose light and love over hate, a very rare thing for a demon to do.

More than anything else, however, he's always been a fixture of the series, offering sage advice to the main characters, because that was the only way he could fight demons.

The Central Question: Who is Mashu'ra, Really?

Of course all of those details are just facts, without much explanation as to how so lowly a demon could know so much and that's what this novel will begin to delve into.

However, I'll give you a few hints, which shouldn't spoil anything too big: Mashu'ra has been hiding so many things, but not because he didn't trust his allies. No, it was merely a matter of trying to leave his old life behind, to never look back, to live in the warmth and light of a better world.

That's because he never wanted to go home to the literal Hell he was summoned from. In point of fact, he was terrified of the possibility ever coming to pass.

At the end of the previous volume, that became exceptionally clear as he was hauled out of the magic hat of Artemis, to translate some demon writing, but as soon as he read the words, "Lady Oz’gozod sends her regards." he screamed and scrabbled his way back toward the hat he'd been pulled from, demonstrating a level of naked terror like nothing his friends had ever seen. He almost made it, only to shot by a high-powered rifle at the last moment, which killed him.

You might think that would be the end of his sad tale, but it isn't, because he was magically summoned to Earth as a spiritual projection and part of that means a free ticket home upon death, with little more than some awful pain to endure.

The trouble is, it also means he returned to the very place he left behind: the clutches of his first Master, Lady Oz'gozod, also known as The Imp Queen. She's also the head of the Torture Department of the Inquisitor's Office of Castigation City, which loosely passes for what demons might term justice (mostly hauling people off the street to be tortured, sometimes at a mere whim).

When Mashu'ra finally came to, it was to the worst possible nightmare: trapped in a tiny cell, at the mercy of Oz'gozod, one of the most vile demons that ever lived.

I held back from pulling this plot thread for a long time, but in the previous two books, Mashu'ra's role had grown smaller and it was time to give him some major character development.

I'm reminded of Malcom Reynolds from Firefly being tortured by that crazy fellow obsessed with pain and really "knowing" people, by holding them over the volcano, so to speak.

Sadly, that may be what lies in store for Mashu'ra, but along the way, you will get to know him in a way you never have. You will learn how he knows so much about demonic politics and you will learn exactly why a mere imp became a force even the highest ranks of infernal politics came to respect. He's always had hidden depths and now, it's time to reveal them.

Unfortunately, his situation seems completely hopeless, because there's no way his human allies would ever open a portal to Hell, just to rescue one demon, right? He's convinced of that fact, because it's plain and clear to him.

Meanwhile, on Earth...

Artemis is completely distraught, because the oldest friend she's got is in enemy hands, a situation she can't and won't accept, because he's more than an ally: he's her brother. Not a blood-relation, but that's how she feels about him. Fortunately, her friends are behind her and she's been tasked with planning a full-scale invasion of Hell itself.

It's not entirely unprecedented, however, because she once led a small rescue mission to do exactly that and she knows the lay of the land, though her information is eighty years out of date. On the other hand, the Ashen Blades recently made an interesting ally, by helping a demon to begin repenting for her numerous sins and she was one of the seven arch demons that rules Hell, namely Lust.

So, it's not impossible, but it's going to take serious magic and firepower, if only the various militaries of Earth can be convinced it's a good idea to finally strike back in the invisible war humanity has been a part of since before the dawn of civilization.

Then there's the trouble of sealing the portal when they're done, which requires something equivalent to nuclear ordnance, but there may be a bit of luck there, because rumor has it their black market arms dealer knows something about a collection of missing Russian suitcase nukes...

This is when the conflict between man and demon really heats up and it's going to be taken to the next level, because it's time for a little payback! It's time to bloody the nose of the biggest bully in the multiverse!

Interested? Here's Where You Find the Book!



 The faithful companion of Artemis Watson, a kitten-shaped demon named Mashu'ra, was killed in the line of duty. He's always lived in her hat and he's like a brother to her, leaving her deeply upset and unable to calm down.

However, there's a slim ray of hope, which from another perspective becomes a ray of horror: the imp originally arrived on Earth through summoning magic and dying merely returned him to Hell. Unfortunately, nearly every demon in the place knows he's a turncoat and the arch-nemesis of Artemis, Vogerath, has been waiting centuries for this to happen.

The Ashen Blades immediately tried a summoning spell to retrieve their friend, but another imp appeared in place of Mashu'ra, informing the kitten's friends who has him. If they want him back, they'll have to visit Hell and promise an unacceptable measure of cooperation that would surely doom the human race.

Meanwhile, Mashu'ra endures a nightmarish ordeal at the hands of his old boss, starting with magically-enhanced interrogation, then torture and brainwashing, using means both magical and mundane.

Artemis and her friends mount a desperate rescue mission and open a portal to that infernal world. Artemis leads the way, with military backup, using all of her power to cut a blood-soaked path through Hell, to reach the Outcast Detention Center, where Mashu'ra is held captive.

Will she be in time to rescue her dear friend before it's too late? Will she manage to save his soul, or will she rescue only his body? Will Mashu'ra ever be the same again?
