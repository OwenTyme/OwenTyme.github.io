Saddling the Storm

Track five of Elementalist is titled Saddling the Storm, in which the Hunter faces the Air Spirit in the plains of Kanans, on a random rural route somewhere near Wichita, during which she had the story of Pecos Bill on her mind. That's the reason her magic clothes embraced a more Old West vibe than usual.

Saddling the Storm us based on events from Demon for President!.
