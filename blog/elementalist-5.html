Elementalist #5

October 12, 2026 — Owen Tyme

Elementalist is an album of mostly heavy metal that I'm working on as a companion to my Ashen Blades series of novels.

Goodbye, Dear Friend

Track six of Elementalist is titled Penance of Metal, in which the Metal spirit feels nothing but guilt about killing Clayton Simmons and considers how to make things right, eventually deciding to seek out the Hunter, to serve her as a form of penance and during their second encounter, she accepts the Metal Spirit.

Goodbye, Dear Friend is based on events from Demon for President! and She Seeks Peace.
