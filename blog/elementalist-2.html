Elementalist #2

October 04, 2026 — Owen Tyme

Elementalist is an album of mostly heavy metal that I'm working on as a companion to my Ashen Blades series of novels.

You can find the previous blog entry here.

On the Subject of Videos

I've been experimenting with making simple videos this week, using just a static image of the cover of the album, beside an AI-generated image of something appropriate based on the source novel/novella. If you'd like to see the videos for previous tracks, they'll be at the end of this blog post.

A Watery Grave

Track four of Elementalist is titled A Watery Grave, in which the Hunter goes to a summer camp infested with demons, to wipe them out, but along the way, the Water Spirit notices the Wood Spirit stuck inside her mind and attacks with intent to kill. It turns into a real nasty series of fights.

A Watery Grave us based on events from She Goes to Summer Camp, which is sold bundled as part of She Goes to War.
