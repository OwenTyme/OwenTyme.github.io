Burning, Broken Heart

Track ten of Elementalist is titled Earth Wakes and Quakes, in which the Earth Spirit fully wakes to find the Hunter in Japan, who has many of Earth's siblings inside her mind. Earth is so offended, she stays awake to plot revenge. When the time comes to strike, the entire world shakes and Earth seeks to turn the Hunter into a lowly worm.

Burning, Broken Heart is based on events from Scarlet Eye: She Hunts Witches, which hasn't been published yet, because I'm currently in the process of editing it.
